Clinic Hours

We are open Monday–Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and the following public holidays: New Year's Day, Independence Day, and Christmas Day.

Appointment Policy

Appointments can be booked online, by phone, or in person. We ask patients to arrive 10 minutes early for new-patient paperwork. Same-day appointments are limited and subject to availability.

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a no-show fee.

Insurance and Payment

We accept most major insurers, including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. We also accept self-pay patients and offer payment plans for eligible cases. Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID to your appointment.

Walk-in Policy

We accept walk-ins for minor urgent issues on a limited basis; walk-in wait times vary depending on scheduled appointments. For non-urgent visits we recommend booking ahead.

Contact and Location

Our clinic is located at 123 Health St., Suite 200. Call us at (555) 123-4567 or email hello@clinic.example to book or ask questions.

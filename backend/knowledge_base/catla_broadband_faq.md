## Technical Troubleshooting
- **Red PON (Passive Optical Network) Light**: Indicates fiber attenuation or cable cut.
  - Step 1: Ensure yellow fiber optic patch cord is not bent or unplugged.
  - Step 2: Log a technician visit ticket.
- **Red LOS (Loss of Signal) Light**: Optical signal from area exchange interrupted.
  - Step 1: Power cycle ONT router (turn off for 30 seconds, then restart).
  - Step 2: If red light persists after 3 minutes, our backend creates an automatic line check ticket.
- **Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet**:
  - Check if 2.4GHz / 5GHz band is selected.
  - Reset DNS to 8.8.8.8 and 1.1.1.1.
